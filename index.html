🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Numora Brain Challenge handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

Numora Brain Challenge is designed as an offline brain-training and math puzzle game. We respect your privacy and aim to keep data collection to a minimum.

2. Personal Information

Numora Brain Challenge does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, contacts, or location.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress
  • XP and level information
  • Achievements
  • Daily streak information
  • Saved questions
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Local statistics and session history

This information is used to provide the app's gameplay and progress features.

4. Internet and Tracking

Numora Brain Challenge is designed to work offline. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs in its current version.

5. Advertising ID

The app does not request or use the Google Advertising ID for advertising or tracking.

6. Device Permissions

The app does not require access to sensitive device information such as:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Photos or personal media

7. Children's Privacy

The app is designed as a general educational and brain-training game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Data Removal

Because gameplay data is stored locally, you may remove it by using the app's reset options, clearing the app's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

9. Data Security

We aim to minimize privacy risks by keeping gameplay data local and by avoiding unnecessary collection of personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be reflected on this page together with an updated revision date.
